# Index — Jacques Vallée, *Messengers of Deception*

Reading notes on Jacques Vallée's *Messengers of Deception* (1979), thirteen chapters covering the foreword/prologue through the conclusion, epilogue, and appendix.

- [00 — Prologue: A Dream World Far Off](00-foreword-and-prologue.md) — Every institution hides its ignorance about UFOs behind rhetoric, and the phenomenon may be technology designed to manipulate belief.

## Part One: The Contacts

- [01 — The Case Against the Spacecraft](01-the-case-against-the-spacecraft.md) — Five converging arguments show UFOs aren't extraterrestrial spacecraft but staged, hologram-like appearances engineered to induce belief.
- [02 — The Bandwagon from Outer Space](02-the-bandwagon-from-outer-space.md) — Whether UFOs are physically real matters less than the social consequences of contact belief, which manipulating groups actively promote.
- [03 — The Deception](03-the-deception.md) — A contactee's teleportation and impossible "revealed" motor suggest UFOs are consciousness-altering technology, making the subject Intelligence work, not Science.
- [04 — Why Do They Keep Staring at the Walls?](04-why-do-they-keep-staring-at-the-walls.md) — Comparing the H.I.M. cult and contactee Jacques Bordas shows belief in metamorphic contact can genuinely, unsettlingly transform believers.

## Part Two: The Manipulations

- [05 — The Political Overtones](05-the-political-overtones.md) — Infiltrating French and American UFO cults reveals recurring themes of intellectual abdication, racism, and totalitarian utopia beneath channeled "wisdom."
- [06 — The Venusian Candidate](06-the-venusian-candidate.md) — The contactee subculture already reaches for real political power, seeking legitimacy through claimed cosmic endorsement of presidents and platforms.
- [07 — Seraphic Transports and Benevolent Programmers](07-seraphic-transports-and-benevolent-programmers.md) — The "Order of Melchizedek" belief system predates UFOs by decades and keeps recurring, releasing forces of disorder rather than love.
- [08 — The Puppets](08-the-puppets.md) — Contactees are puppets of human Manipulators who exploit occult groups and mind-control-capable technology to stage cosmic mythology for political control.

## Part Three: The Stratagems

- [09 — A Cow for NORAD](09-a-cow-for-norad.md) — Cattle mutilations share UFO landings' social signature and, concentrated near NORAD, look like a deliberate message calling officialdom's bluff.
- [10 — Intelligence Reports](10-intelligence-reports.md) — A collapsed jailhouse "occult" confession and precedents for staged counter-insurgency mythology suggest mutilations and UFO belief are engineered, not cult crime.
- [11 — The Stratagem Theories](11-the-stratagem-theories.md) — Drawing on WWII deception operations, Vallée argues it isn't the UFO phenomenon that's manipulated but belief in it, by a human control system.

- [12 — Conclusion / Epilogue: A Sociologist's Reaction / Appendix: Statement on the UFO Phenomenon](12-conclusion-epilogue-appendix.md) — Science can't investigate designed phenomena; belief in contact is already reshaping society, a choice between threat and opportunity.
