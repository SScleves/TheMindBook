# Ch 17 — Aliens among Us

## In one paragraph
Peebles traces how the three separate strands of the early-1980s flying saucer myth — abductions, crashed saucers, and cattle mutilations ("mutes") — were woven into a single, darker "alien myth," and how that myth then dissolved into conspiracy politics. He locates the origin in Albuquerque physicist Paul Bennewitz, whose translated "alien signals," malevolent Grays, secret government/alien treaty, and Dulce underground base supplied the template before he suffered a breakdown; from there the trail runs through AFOSI agent Richard Doty and researcher William Moore, the "Project Aquarius Document," the presidential "Briefing Paper" shown to Linda Moulton Howe, and the 1987 release of the "Majestic 12" briefing document. Peebles then lays out, item by item, the stylistic, procedural, and factual evidence he says shows the MJ-12 papers are forgeries — culminating in Peebles's report of Philip Klass's demonstration that the Truman memo's signature was identical to one on another 1947 letter and that the memo was typed on a machine first sold in 1963. The chapter tracks the myth's radicalization through the "whistleblowers" John Lear and Robert Lazar to Milton William Cooper, whose MJ-12 narrative absorbs the Kennedy assassination, CIA drug running, AIDS, Moon and Mars colonies, and finally the *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*. Peebles argues the underground bases close a fifty-year circle back to Shaver's Deros, and that the alien myth is "intensely political, with something for every extremist." He ends with a numbered creed-like summary of the alien myth as it stood in 1987–1993.

## Core claims
- Peebles claims that in the early 1980s the flying saucer myth had three distinct threads — abductions, crashed saucers, and the mutes — and that these were interwoven into a new "alien myth" that would "both encompass and overshadow the old flying saucer myth," before itself becoming "submerged in a witch's brew of fascist conspiracy theories, hate, and paranoia."
- He claims the alien myth emerged from the mute myth and, "in retrospect, it is clear," was "a product of the troubled mind of Dr. Paul Bennewitz."
- He warns that past Bennewitz "one enters a swamp of suspicions, hoaxes, claims of disinformation, deception, and lies — a place where every source is tainted, 'official documents' are not what they are claimed, and nothing is as it seems." This is his stated evidentiary posture for the whole chapter.
- Peebles claims the terms "Project Aquarius" and "MJ-12" first appeared in the document Doty gave Moore in February 1981, and that these two terms would come to "dominate and shape the flying saucer myth."
- He reports Moore admitted the Project Aquarius Document "was not authentic, in that he had retyped it and added an official-looking date stamp."
- He argues the MJ-12 briefing document and the associated Cutler memo and Truman memo are forgeries, citing style, classification-marking, procedural, and factual errors (detailed below), and notes "by the early 1990s, most believers had come to the conclusion the documents were forgeries. Yet, MJ-12 had taken on a life of its own."
- He argues MJ-12 belongs to a long American theme of distrust of "secret societies" as "elitist cabals," functioning as a mystery religion "in which levels of security clearances replace levels of initiation," and draws an explicit parallel to *The Protocols of the Elders of Zion*, noting five of the twelve named MJ-12 "members" had Jewish-sounding names "(with Donald Menzel as Judas)."
- He claims belief in government "disinformation" gave the myth an unfalsifiable defense: "Whenever a document or UFO sighting proved false, the situation could still be salvaged by saying it was a carefully conceived government disinformation effort."
- He argues Roswell behaves like a myth rather than a historical problem: more information made the picture less clear, producing rival mutually contradictory versions (Moore vs. Randle/Schmitt vs. Friedman/Berliner; CUFOS vs. MUFON).
- He claims most of the "details" about the Aurora aircraft originated with people who believed black aircraft were reverse-engineered from crashed saucers, and reports a senior government official told him that after an investigation "no evidence of its existence was found" and that officially no such aircraft as described "had been developed or flown."
- He claims Cooper's material can be traced back to Bennewitz and Doty despite Cooper's claim to have learned it in the Navy in 1970–1973, and that Cooper's Moon claims come "straight out of Adamski" while "Alternative 3" came from a BBC "April Fools" documentary.
- He argues the alien myth is "intensely political, with something for every extremist — Ultra-Right, Ultra-Left, and Nihilist," and demonstrates the point by mapping Cooper's claims onto each.
- He argues the underground bases "bring the circle to a close": after five decades the myth returns "where we began — with the Deros in their underground world of madness, pain and fear."

## Concepts
- **alien myth** — Peebles's name for the composite mythology that fused abductions, crashed saucers, and mutes into one story of malevolent Grays, a secret government treaty, underground bases, and technological exchange; it encompassed and overshadowed the older flying saucer myth and then merged with political conspiracy theory.
- **Grays / Highs (Talls, Blonds)** — Bennewitz's two alien types: the malevolent "Grays," to whom he applied the term "Extraterrestrial Biological Entities" (EBE), and the friendlier "Nordic-type" Highs.
- **Project Beta** — Bennewitz's effort to detect the low-frequency electromagnetic signals he believed the aliens used to control abductees; he wrote a personal-computer program to "translate" them and later claimed the aliens had helped him.
- **the secret treaty** — The recurring core of the myth: the U.S. government allows the aliens mutes, abductions, and underground bases in exchange for alien technology and weapons. In Bennewitz's version the aliens then double-cross the government with defective weapons.
- **Project Aquarius Document** — A supposed November 7, 1980, AFOSI teletype given by Doty to Moore in February 1981; the first appearance of both "Project Aquarius" and "MJ TWELVE."
- **presidential briefing paper** — Sheets titled "Briefing Paper for the President of the United States of America" that Doty showed Howe on April 9, 1983, describing multiple crashes, the captive alien "Ebe," 25,000 years of DNA manipulation, and a list of code-named projects.
- **Project Garnet / Sigma / Snowbird / Pounce / Aquarius** — The code-named projects appearing in the briefing documents: Garnet (human evolution — all questions answered, project closed; called "Bando" in the Executive Briefing version), Sigma (communication with the aliens), Snowbird (research and test flight of recovered alien craft), Pounce (evaluation of UFO information on space technology), Aquarius (overall accumulation of information on the aliens).
- **MJ-12 / "Majestic 12"** — The alleged twelve-member group set up by Truman after Roswell; the briefing document names its membership and briefs President-elect Eisenhower. Cooper later said "MJ" stood for "Majority." Peebles's endnote lists candidate real-world inspirations for the phrase (see below).
- **Cutler memo** — An unsigned carbon copy found by Moore and Shandera at the National Archives in 1985, dated July 14, 1954, referring to an "NSC/MJ-12 Special Studies Project" briefing; taken by some as the "smoking gun."
- **disinformation** — Doty's claim (from January 1983) that the government infiltrated UFO groups and fed them false material; Peebles says the claim "grew over the coming years" and became the myth's all-purpose escape hatch.
- **the whistleblowers** — Peebles's label for the final stage: people claiming secret insider knowledge of the aliens — John Lear, then Robert Lazar, then Milton William Cooper.
- **"Grand Deception"** — Lear's term for the aliens' betrayal of MJ-12; Peebles maps it onto the damnation motif in the myth's Hell-like underground bases.
- **Alternatives 1, 2, and 3** — In Cooper's account, the Jason Society's three responses to a 1957 study predicting the Earth could not support life by 2000: nuclear holes blown in the stratosphere (rejected); vast underground cities for selected survivors; colonies on the Moon and Mars. All three included birth control, sterilization, and deadly microbes to cut population.
- **technological mystery religion** — Peebles's reading of the myth's structure: security clearances stand in for grades of initiation, and the "sacred places" are known by code names — "words of power" — MJ-12, Area 51, and S-4.
- **conspiracy subculture / "Nazification of America"** — The political matrix Peebles says the myth entered: a loose network of theorists "for whom every death is suspicious, and every little incident has a labyrinthine political, social, and historical context," with the "Nazification of America" a common theme.

## Experiments, examples & case studies
- **Paul Bennewitz, Albuquerque (from 1979)** — Physicist, president of a small electronics company, and APRO investigator. The Lorenzens believed he was "prone to make great leaps of logic on the basis of incomplete data" and had decided what he would find before investigating. In August 1979 he and Dr. Leo Sprinkle investigated an abduction case: a mother and young son driving a rural highway near Cimarron, New Mexico; over three hypnosis sessions the woman said they observed two or more UFOs and a calf being abducted, that she and her son were themselves abducted and taken to an underground base, that she saw the calf being mutilated, a vat of body parts floating in a liquid, another vat holding the body of a human male, an examination, and small metallic objects implanted in both her and her son. By August 1979 Bennewitz concluded the implants controlled abductees and that alien control signals could be detected — "Project Beta"; in late 1979 he reported to APRO that he had picked up low-frequency electromagnetic signals from UFOs and wrote a program to translate them.
- **Bennewitz's mythology by mid-1982** — Two alien types (Grays/EBEs and Highs/Talls/Blonds); the Grays mutilating cattle for blood and organs to build humanoids by gene-splicing, taking material from live cattle to maintain DNA supply; hundreds of thousands to millions of people abducted and implanted; a secret treaty granting the Grays mutes, abductions, and a base near "Archuteta Peak" outside Dulce, New Mexico (site of a late-1970s mute wave) plus three other areas, in exchange for technology and weapons; the Grays violating the treaty with defective weapons; battles already fought between the Grays and the U.S. military; the intent to reduce humanity to slaves or cattle. [OCR: the scan prints "Archuteta Peak"; no corrected reading is given here because the chapter supplies none.]
- **Bennewitz's crusade and collapse** — He wrote letters and made phone calls "by the hundreds," and with his degree and compelling style influenced other UFOlogists, notably Linda Moulton Howe and John Lear (son of Learjet inventor Bill Lear). Others were unimpressed: his photos of a crashed UFO, burned wreckage, and a base entrance showed, to others, "rocks, trees, and shadows"; his program assigned words and whole sentences to individual signal pulses, and when the text elements were broken up, reshuffled, and reassembled the "new" message came out the same. Hynek and the Lorenzens expressed doubts. Bennewitz grew erratic, claiming aliens came through the walls at night and injected him with chemicals; he suffered a mental breakdown and was hospitalized.
- **Doty's report and the Project Aquarius Document (1980–81)** — Sgt. Richard C. Doty, AFOSI special agent at Kirtland AFB, wrote a September 1980 report on unidentified lights over the Kirtland test range. By February 1981 he had a working relationship with William Moore, then on APRO's board, and gave him the "Project Aquarius Document": an apparent November 7, 1980, Secret teletype from AFOSI headquarters in Washington to Kirtland, analyzing Bennewitz's UFO photos ("saucer shaped, approximate diameter 37 feet… a trilateral insignia") and stating that the USAF is "no longer publicly active in UFO research" while "[s]everal other government agencies, lead by NASA, actively investigates [sic] legitimate sightings through covert cover," one such cover being the "UFO Reporting Center, U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey, Rockville MD 20853"; that the results of Project Aquarius are Top Secret with restricted access to "MJ TWELVE," and that the Bennewitz case was being monitored by NASA/INS.
- **The Aquarius Project novel (1982–83)** — In January 1982 Moore told ex-*National Enquirer* reporter Robert Pratt about Aquarius and MJ-12, sourcing it to a "Deep Throat." Judging it unprovable, they agreed to publish it as fiction: Moore supplying information, Pratt writing, Doty understood as a silent third party with manuscript review rights, the central figure an AFOSI agent. Finished in late 1983; never published.
- **The KPIX-TV exposure (1982)** — Moore, hired in late spring 1982 as consultant on a UFO special, brought in television producer Jaime Shandera and nuclear physicist/lecturer Stanton Friedman in June, and supplied KPIX with the Aquarius Document. On August 6, 1982, KPIX's Bob Peters asked the Air Force about it; an AFOSI investigation called it a forgery with numerous flaws in style and format. Moore then admitted he had retyped it and added an official-looking date stamp.
- **Doty's January 1983 briefing** — Meeting KPIX producer Ron Lakis, FOIA lawyer Peter Gersten, and Moore, Doty said OSI was deeply involved with UFOs, that he had investigated UFOs for five or six years, and that he had access to presidential UFO briefing papers. His claims echoed Bennewitz's (secret treaty, mutes and abductions for technology, NSA trying to communicate with the aliens) except that Doty's aliens were benevolent. He named three crashes with bodies recovered — Roswell, one in the 1950s, one in the 1960s — and said the government had infiltrated UFO groups with "disinformation" and was "programming" the public through movies and television.
- **Linda Howe and the briefing paper (April 9, 1983)** — Proposing an HBO documentary, *UFOS: The E.T. Factor*, Howe met Doty at the Kirtland AFOSI office; he gave her sheets titled "Briefing Paper for the President of the United States of America," saying his superiors wanted her to read it and ask questions but not take notes. Contents: crashes in 1946, 1947, 1949 and several in the early 1950s (sites including Aztec, New Mexico; Kingman, Arizona; northern Mexico; two separate crashes at Roswell); radar disabling UFO guidance systems; craft and bodies taken to Los Alamos and Wright-Patterson; EBEs with gray skin, long arms, clawlike nails webbed between four fingers, and noses and ears reduced to small holes. In 1949 a UFO crashed near Roswell with six aliens, one alive; an Air Force officer took the survivor, "Ebe," to Los Alamos, where over three years — until his death on June 18, 1952 — he described his civilization and the aliens' role in human evolution, telepathically and verbally; his home was a planet in a binary system about fifty-five light years away. The EBEs had come for at least 25,000 years to manipulate the DNA of terrestrial primates and other life (intervals Howe recalled: 25,000, 15,000, 5,000, 2,500 years ago), and "Two thousand years ago extraterrestrials created a being" to teach humans love and nonviolence. The paper closed with Projects Garnet, Sigma, Snowbird, and Aquarius, noting some Aquarius data had been used in the U.S. space program, and mentioned another group of aliens, the "Talls," of whom Doty said, "They tolerate each other," adding that there was some kind of friction between them. In an endnote Peebles argues the paper's provenance is derivative: with the exception of the second Roswell crash and "Ebe," it "seems to have been drawn from Stringfield's articles published in *Flying Saucer Review*" — the EBE physical description is identical and both refer to the same crashes — while the reincarnation material "seem[s] to have been drawn from the contactee myth"; he adds that "[i]t seems redundant to investigate the claim that the U.S. government has secret research teams on a planet 55 light years away."
- **The promised film and the Holloman meeting** — Doty told Howe she was being shown the paper because she would soon receive several thousand feet of film of crashed saucers, alien bodies, the live Ebe, and a human/alien meeting at Holloman AFB at 6:00 A.M. on April 25, 1964 — one UFO landing, two hovering, greeted by the officer who befriended Ebe and two scientists; alien bodies returned and, by implication, an "exchange program." The Socorro "landing" had been an error in time and location; the second attempt "came off without a hitch." In the end no film was released, for "political reasons."
- **The Mexican restaurant conversation** — Doty told Howe the "Ebans" lived like Pueblo Indians in houses carved from soil and rock on a hot desert world; asked whether the binary stars were Zeta Reticula (as in the Fish map) he refused to say; asked how anyone knew how they lived, he answered, "Something like that. You might say we have research scientists studying their planets like they study ours." No one knew why Ebe died; they had tried to save him by signaling the Ebans, and when he died the Air Force officer cried — Doty compared it to *E.T.* Asked whether Ebe said anything about God: "He said our souls recycle, that reincarnation is real. It's the machinery of the universe."
- **Victor Marchetti's 1979 interview** — In *Second Look* (May 1979) the ex-CIA official and co-author of *The CIA and the Cult of Intelligence* noted that released CIA documents showed only routine interest in UFO reports — "However," he noted, "few such reports were released—and that implies a cover-up!" — and theorized an international conspiracy among governments to conceal contact, lest political, legal, religious, economic, and social institutions "become meaningless in the mind of the public." Peebles marks this as the first forging of the link between political nihilism and UFOs.
- **"The Occult, MIB's, UFOs and Assassinations" (*Stigmata*, 1986)** — Built the UFO/conspiracy link around Fred Lee Crisman of the Maury Island hoax: Harold Dahl "vanished" after talking with Kenneth Arnold; Crisman recalled to Air Force duty and sent to Alaska then Greenland; Davidson and Brown "killed" after their investigation; slag samples switched and others stolen from Ray Palmer's office; Tacoma reporter Paul Lance dead soon after helping Arnold; Arnold nearly killed by engine failure. It further claimed one of the three "mystery tramps" arrested in Dallas on November 22, 1963, had been "identified" as Crisman, who was also called before the New Orleans grand jury in Jim Garrison's investigation. Peebles answers in an endnote that Crisman "was the perfect suspect" precisely because his testimony was never released and he is now dead, and that Garrison's investigation "was worthless, relying on hearsay, nonexistent 'links,' and spurious 'unanswered questions'" — most of the "suspects" were dead by the time Garrison sought indictments, and the only man brought to trial, Clay Shaw, was acquitted in short order. The two Air Force officers named, Davidson and Brown, had died after finishing their investigation.
- **The "Executive Briefing/Subject: Project Aquarius" pages (1986)** — Apparent pages of a ten-page Top Secret briefing, cover labeled "EXECUTIVE CORRESPONDENCE," with a list of UFO project code names. Peebles quotes the redacted text on Project Sigma (established within another project in 1954, separate from 1976; first primitive communication with the aliens in 1959; a USAF intelligence officer's roughly three-hour meeting with two aliens in the New Mexico desert on April 25, 1964; continuing at an Air Force base in New Mexico), Project Snowbird (established 1972, mission to test-fly a recovered alien aircraft, continuing in Nevada), and a project established in 1968 to evaluate UFO information on space technology — "PROJECT POUNCE continues." Moore claimed he had been shown the document and allowed to photograph it. It also asserted that "EBE reported that 2,000 years ago his ancestors planted a human creature on earth" and that releasing this "would cause a world-wide panic." Differences from Howe's version: only three crashes (1947 with four bodies, 1949 when EBE was captured, and 1958 with a saucer found abandoned in the Utah desert); EBE's home world identified as Zeta Reticula, which Doty had refused to confirm to Howe; and the human-evolution study called "Bando" rather than Howe's "Garnet." [OCR: the extract's paragraph numbering prints as "3," and "4," with blanked PROWORD fields.]
- **The MJ-12 briefing document (released May 29, 1987)** — Moore, Shandera, and Friedman released "Briefing Document: Operation Majestic 12," purportedly a preliminary briefing for President-elect Eisenhower dated "18 November, 1952," briefing officer Adm. Roscoe H. Hillenkoetter (MJ-1). Named original members: Hillenkoetter, Vannevar Bush, Defense Secretary James V. Forrestal, Gen. Nathan F. Twining, Gen. Hoyt S. Vandenberg, Detlev Bronk, Jerome Hunsaker, Sidney W. Souers, Gordon Gray, Donald Menzel, Gen. Robert M. Montague, and Lloyd V. Berkner — all dead by 1987 — with CIA director Gen. Walter B. Smith replacing Forrestal on "01 August, 1950." The text describes the July 7, 1947, recovery operation, four small human-like beings apparently ejected before the craft exploded and found dead and badly decomposed about two miles east of the wreckage after roughly a week; a short-range reconnaissance craft judged so from its size and lack of provisioning; Bronk's team's tentative conclusion of November 30, 1947, proposing the term "Extra-terrestrial Biological Entities"; no wings, propellers, jets, wiring, or vacuum tubes; undecipherable writing; and a second object impacting on "06 December, 1950" in the El Indio–Guerrero area of the Texas–Mexico "boder," almost totally incinerated, its remains taken to the A.E.C. facility at Sandia. Eight attachments were listed but only one included: a September 24, 1947, Truman memo to Forrestal establishing MJ-12. Moore said the document arrived on a roll of undeveloped 35 mm film sent to Shandera in December 1984, and that they spent two years trying to authenticate it.
- **The Cutler memo and the National Archives' objections** — Found in 1985: an unsigned carbon dated July 14, 1954, from presidential special assistant Robert Cutler to General Twining on the "NSC/MJ-12 Special Studies Project," moving an MJ-12 SSP briefing into the already-scheduled White House meeting of July 16. Moore said it "unquestionably verifies the existence of an 'MJ-12' group in 1954"; to others it was "too good to be true." The National Archives noted: no Top Secret register number despite the Top Secret classification; the marking "Top Secret Restricted Information," a classification not used at the NSC until the Nixon administration nearly two decades later; no government watermark (Cutler's NSC carbons used onionskin with an eagle watermark); no NSC meeting on July 16, 1954, and no NSC records on MJ-12, Majestic, UFOs, flying saucers, or flying disks; no entry in Eisenhower's Appointment Books, which listed even off-the-record meetings by time and participants; and Cutler was not in Washington or the United States on July 14, 1954 — he was visiting military bases in Europe and North Africa from July 3 to 15. A genuine memo of the same date was signed by James S. Lay. Peebles adds in an endnote that the Cutler memo "is generally thought to have been smuggled into the Archives," and that even if its inconsistencies could be excused it would only prove that something called "MJ-12" once existed — the memo says nothing about UFOs or about what MJ-12 dealt with, and "could equally be about the breaking of a Soviet code system or reports of a Soviet 'agent-in-place.'"
- **Peebles's evidence that the briefing paper is a forgery** — (1) Date format: "01 August," "07 August," "06 December" — Peebles writes that he "has never seen an '0' prefix before a single digit date in the text of a government document," and that the document's backers have offered no examples; the comma after the month is also wrong ("6 December 1950"). Both Klass and CAUS noted Moore had used this same date format since the fall of 1983 in personal letters, papers, and retyped official documents. (2) Classification markings: CAUS found the lettering of "TOP SECRET/MAJIC EYES ONLY" identical in style to Moore's return-address rubber stamp, which used removable lettering — the "I" is raised slightly in "MAJIC" and in the "WILLIAM" and "OLIVE" of Moore's address. The copying warning does not match real 1952 wording, and Top Secret documents carry page-count statements ("This document has ___ pages," "Page ___ of ___ Pages") for periodic checks; the MJ-12 briefing has none. (3) Factual: "Roswell Army Air Base (now Walker Field)" should be Roswell Army Air Field and Walker AFB — "Army Air Base" was not used after 1943 and "Field" not after 1947. (4) Style: "media" rather than "press," "impacted" as a verb, "Extra-terrestrial" rather than "alien" — terms not popular until the 1960s — plus the misspelling "boder," non-American punctuation (", and ". rather than ," and ."), the hyphenated "homo-sapiens" (also in the Aquarius briefing paper), and missing commas. (5) From Peebles's endnote on CAUS's work: the official-looking "EXECUTIVE CORRESPONDENCE" was "nothing more than a clear plastic cover used to transfer and file documents," and the Truman Library told CAUS the Executive Order number given in the briefing document, #092447, was bogus — the EOs issued in September and October 1947 were #9891–9896, while 092447 is simply the date 09-24-47. Peebles states in the same endnotes that his own analysis of the Majestic 12 documents rests on his experience as an aerospace historian since 1977, in which he has examined thousands of pages of declassified documents ranging up to Top Secret (Codeword) and going back to the late 1940s.
- **Hillenkoetter and Menzel as internal evidence** — Hillenkoetter was CIA director from May 1, 1947, to October 7, 1950, yet appears as MJ-12 chairman in November 1952; Peebles notes one newspaper account calls him "then CIA Director" and suggests "the hoaxer may have made the same mistake." After retiring from the Navy in May 1957, Hillenkoetter joined the board of NICAP. Menzel wrote three anti-UFO books; James Moseley, who knew him, said "he made Phil Klass look like a hard-core believer!" and found evidence that Menzel and Hillenkoetter did not know each other — impossible had they served together. Peebles concludes Menzel "had been included as an act of revenge for his past activities."
- **Klass and the Truman memo (early 1990)** — Peebles reports that in early 1990 Klass showed the September 24, 1947, Truman memo to be a forgery: the "Harry Truman" signature was identical to the one on an October 1, 1947, letter to Vannevar Bush, down to a distinctive skid mark on the "H" — "It is impossible for two signatures to be identical." A document examiner told Klass the memo was typed on a Smith-Corona typewriter first sold in 1963, fifteen years after its purported date. A further endnote records a separate FBI finding: on October 11, 1988, FBI Special Agent Bill Vinikus told CAUS director Peter Gersten the Bureau had determined the MJ-12 papers were not genuine classified government documents, the agent adding that he found it difficult to believe they had been presented to the public as real.
- **1987 as a publishing year** — Alongside the MJ-12 documents came Budd Hopkins's *Intruders*, Whitley Strieber's *Communion* (his torment at the hands of alien "visitors"), which reached number 1 on the *New York Times* best seller list by May 1987, and *Light Years*, on contactee Eduard Meier, who claimed to have travelled back in time and met Jesus Christ. Peebles calls it a level of interest not seen since the 1973 flap aftermath — "(All that was missing were UFO sightings.)"
- **UFO Cover-Up? Live! (October 14, 1988)** — The telecast featured "Falcon" and "Condor," claimed to be two of Moore's "Deep Throat" sources, faces hidden and voices disguised. Falcon: "Presently, as of the year 1988 there is one extraterrestrial being. He's a guest of the United States government." Condor claimed a signed U.S.–alien agreement including a Nevada base at "an area called Area 51 or Dreamland" of which "the extraterrestrials have complete control," and added that the aliens enjoy all music, "especially ancient Tibetian style music," and that "their favorite dish or snack is ice cream—especially strawberry." CAUS called it "an amazingly inept move"; Gersten, Howe, and ex-AFOSI agents identified Falcon as Doty.
- **Doty's collapse** — In early 1985, a few weeks after the documents were mailed, Doty was transferred from Kirtland to West Germany on counterintelligence duty. His superiors suspected some of his reports of contacts with communist agents were faked; he flunked a lie-detector test, was removed from AFOSI, and returned to Kirtland in late 1986, spending his final Air Force year as a "food services specialist" managing the mess hall. In a March 3, 1988, letter to CAUS he denied showing Howe the briefing paper and wrote, "I know of no secret Government investigation of UFOs. I have never heard of MJ-12 or any secret Government agency that investigates UFOs." Howe responded with a sworn statement that her account was true.
- **The EBE-3 trip (October 1987)** — Moore claimed his high-level intelligence contacts told him and Shandera to fly to Washington, where someone would take them to a wooded area to interview and film "EBE-3." They went; no one met them.
- **Moore's 1989 MUFON speech** — In a long, rambling statement Moore claimed that since September 1980 he had acted as an unpaid double agent for the government, approached by a "well-placed" individual who claimed to be connected with a government UFO project; "The Falcon" represented people opposed to the cover-up and offered information to end it, with Doty as liaison. In exchange Moore said he provided the government information on APRO and Bennewitz. He further claimed Bennewitz had been the victim of a government disinformation effort — the secret treaties, malevolent aliens, underground bases, technology exchanges, and battles with aliens intended to discredit him, and resulting in his breakdown — and that "at least some, quite possibly a substantial portion" of what Falcon and Condor said on *UFO Cover-Up? Live!* was also disinformation, adding "I am equally convinced that some of it is true." He justified his participation as an attempt to learn about the cover-up. Moseley said the speech "sunk [Moore's] credibility to a new low." CAUS observed: "Given the current sad state of affairs with the credibility of UFOlogy, one wonders whether the government considers UFOlogists important at all!"
- **UFO Crash at Aztec (1987) and Robert Sarbacher** — William S. Steinman and Wendelle C. Stevens published an expanded version of Scully's tale; one anonymous "witness" described the saucer hitting a cliff, another said that if news of the craft's water-driven engine reached the scientific community "that would be the end of the oil industry." A source was Dr. Robert Sarbacher, a one-time Research and Development Board consultant, who told Canadian scientist Wilber Smith in September 1950 that a small group under Vannevar Bush was investigating UFOs, that Scully's book was "substantially correct," and that UFOs were "classified two points higher than the H-bomb… the most highly classified subject in the U.S. government at the present time." In a 1983 letter to Steinman he added that materials from saucer crashes were "extremely light and very tough" and that the aliens were of "very light weight… constructed like certain insects" — while noting, "I had no association with any of the people involved in the recovery and have no knowledge regarding the dates of the recoveries." Peebles's endnote records that the Smith interview "is believed by some to have been the starting point for the MJ-12 forgeries," that Sarbacher's insect-like aliens resemble those in Gerald Heard's 1951 *Is Another World Watching?*, and that the strong-but-light material "sounds like the debris described in *The Roswell Incident*."
- **Peebles's case that Aztec was a hoax** — Peebles holds that the Aztec "crash" "remains a hoax by two con men," citing research done ironically by Moore in the early 1980s: Silas M. Newton had been in trouble with the law as early as 1928; Leo GeBauer had been investigated for violating the White Slave Traffic Act, was a Nazi sympathizer, and used at least eleven aliases. Newton was charged (and acquitted) in 1959 over $125,000 in worthless uranium-mine securities, was under indictment on two counts of grand theft as late as 1970, and at his death in Los Angeles in December 1972 at age 83 faced some 140 civil suits totalling $1,350,000 for salting mines and pumping oil into wells to deceive investors; his estate came to $16,000. GeBauer died in poverty in Colorado in late 1982. Peebles's endnote adds Moore's own reconstruction of where the story came from: a publicity stunt for the film *The Flying Saucer*, whose director/producer/star Mikel Conrad claimed in August 1949 that the film's saucer footage was real and government-supplied (two press agents later resigned and apologized for their unwitting role), plus two Death Valley prospectors who said they had watched a UFO crash in a sand dune and its two crewmen run off, after which the saucer too was gone; Newton, who had known Scully since 1944 and used him for access to Hollywood stars, combined the two. Peebles adds that "witnesses" coming forward to "describe" a crash shown to be a hoax "is a point against other UFO crash stories," and notes that Moore blamed "disinformation" for the Aztec stories — "another example of how useful the concept can be."
- **The three Roswells** — Some 300 witnesses came forward during the 1980s. Berlitz and Moore (1980): the UFO struck by lightning over Mac Brazel's ranch, creating the debris field, then crashing some 150 miles away. Randle and Schmitt, *UFO Crash at Roswell* (1991): the craft came down a few miles from Brazel's debris, was found by aerial reconnaissance, the area sealed by the Army on July 8, 1947, with the saucer and four dead aliens recovered — close to the MJ-12 account. Friedman and Berliner, *Crash at Corona* (1992): two saucers collided in midair, one disintegrating over Brazel's ranch leaving four dead aliens, the second crashing on the Plains of San Augustin with three dead and one survivor — directly counter to the MJ-12 documents, which mention neither a second saucer nor a survivor, though Friedman was the documents' most outspoken defender. Peebles quotes one writer: "Like all good stories, Roswell expands to accommodate whatever you bring to it… detailed enough to seem real, yet fuzzy enough to stay always just beyond the reach of objective proof… Roswell grows a little with each retelling."
- **The Aurora aircraft (late 1980s–1992)** — Reports of a high-altitude, high-speed aircraft over the Mojave in early morning hours, producing a "pulsing" sound, a "linked-sausage-shaped smoke trail," and on takeoff "a sound like the sky ripping"; speculated capabilities mach 5–8 and 250,000 feet. Technical-press articles from December 1989 described an unmanned, elongated diamond-shaped vehicle about 110 feet long and 60 feet wide, surfaced with black ceramic tiles of "scorched, heat-streaked appearance" coated with "a crystalline patina," emitting "a burnt-carbon odor," with rear tiles pockmarked by an external-burning engine — fuel sprayed across the rear fuselage and ignited, the fuselage shape acting as a rocket nozzle ("impulse motor") — and 121 nuclear warheads in individual ports underneath. Later articles said it looked like the XB-70 bomber, later still a large delta. In 1992 the *Los Angeles Times* reported five brief rumbles between June 1991 and April 1992 heard by Los Angeles residents, sonic booms rather than earthquakes, all at 7:00 a.m. on Thursdays; *Time*'s "Grapevine" reported Aurora flying from RAF Machrihanish, Scotland, code-named "Senior Citizen." Peebles reports a senior government official told him the articles caused "a major uproar within the government," that a high-level investigation, despite diligent checking, found no evidence of its existence, and that he officially stated no such aircraft as described had been developed or flown; the same official said the stories had come from UFO believers, which Peebles says was confirmed by a private individual who has long researched black aircraft projects. Peebles notes in an endnote the parallel claim that the B-2 bomber is actually powered by an engine removed from a crashed saucer, and the irony that the twenty B-2s were to be assigned to the 509th Bomb Wing, once based at Roswell AAF.
- **John Lear's statement (December 29, 1987)** — The first whistleblower claimed the "horrible truth" that the U.S. government had sold out the human race to the evil EBEs: during 1969–1971 MJ-12 and the aliens agreed to cover up abductions and mutes in exchange for technology, with MJ-12 given a list of abductees; the secret base was set up at Groom Lake in 1972–1974. The aliens' genetic disorder caused digestive failure, so they used "an enzyme or hormonal secretion" from human and animal mutes, mixed with hydrogen peroxide and spread on their skin; "some of the nation's missing children had been used for secretions and other parts"; "cows and humans are genetically similar. In the event of a national disaster, cow's blood can be used by humans." Between 1979 and 1983 MJ-12 realized its error — first indication "the Dulce massacre" of 1979, in which sixty-six Special Forces soldiers died trying to rescue humans held at Dulce. The "Grand Deception" sent MJ-12 "into utter confusion and panic," and development of an anti-alien weapon continued under the guise of the Strategic Defense Initiative, "which had nothing whatsoever to do with a defense for inbound Russian nuclear missiles." Lear: "Is the more sinister and most probable situation that the invasion is essentially complete and it is all over but the screaming?" and, in interview, "They're going to march us just exactly like the Holocaust."
- **Robert Lazar and Area 51 (1988–1990)** — In July 1988 Lazar, saying he was a scientist working in New Mexico, contacted Lear, who gave him a "five-hour lecture"; in late September Lazar said he had independently verified Lear's information using a classified library. Lear introduced him to KLAS-TV Las Vegas reporter George Knapp, and on November 10, 1989, the station began a series on Area 51 based on Lazar's stories. Lazar claimed he had been shown classified UFO reports, alien autopsy reports and photographs, and saucer photos; that the project was called "Majestic" and run by the Navy; that nine saucers were at "S-4," one holed top and bottom as if hit by a four- or five-inch shell; that he knew they were from outer space when he saw small chairs inside; that he watched test flights in which the underside glowed blue and hissed, then went silent after lift-off; and that the saucers used an antimatter reactor creating an antigravity field, powered by "Element 115." He described oppressive security — being hit in the chest, screamed at, and having M-16s pointed at him. Peebles's holes: inconclusive lie-detector results; vivid recall of alien technology alongside forgotten large blocks of time; unconfirmable educational background; no membership in the American Physical Society or American Nuclear Society; and, in August 1990, a guilty plea to one felony count of pandering, with three years' probation.
- **The Lear/Cooper "Indictment" (early 1989)** — Charged the U.S. government "with murder and treason against the people and Constitution of these United States" over the secret alien treaty, accusing the president, vice president, CIA and NSA directors, certain cabinet members, MJ-12 members, the Jason Society, the directors of the Senior Interagency Group, the national security adviser and others going back to 1953 of a "joint government and alien conspiracy." They demanded the government order the "Alien Nation" to leave the Earth by June 1, 1989, and disclose its alien involvement before April 30, 1989, swearing "upon the Constitution" to persist "until our death" in the name of "all true patriots who have gone before us."
- **Cooper's "The Secret Government" (May 23, 1989)** — Twenty-seven UFO crashes between 1947 and 1953; ninety-one alien bodies and five live aliens recovered; two UFOs recovered near Aztec in 1948 carrying a large number of human body parts; a classification above Top Secret imposed. Forrestal, having begun talking about "the alien problem," was forced to resign, admitted to Bethesda Naval Hospital under pretext of a mental breakdown, and murdered by CIA agents. Truman kept all allies including the Soviet Union informed; the "Bilderburgers" were set up in Geneva to keep the secret and became a secret world government "that now controls everything." Eisenhower's request for Nelson Rockefeller's help was "the biggest mistake Eisenhower ever made for the future of the United States and most probably all of humanity"; within a year MJ-12 ("MJ" for "Majority") was set up, split between government and the Council on Foreign Relations and staffed from the Jason Society (elite scientists), Yale's and Harvard's "Skull and Bones" and "Scroll and Key," and the Trilateral Commission — whose name Cooper claimed derived from the aliens' "Trilateral" flag.
- **Cooper's alien chronology** — 1953: astronomers detect large objects approaching Earth, which enter very high orbit; blond humanlike "Nordics" contact the government to warn against them and against humanity's self-destruction, offering spiritual help at the price of destroying all nuclear weapons — refused. 1954: "Large-nosed Grays" land at Holloman AFB, saying they come from a planet of the red giant Betelgeuse and need a new home; a second landing at Edwards AFB with Eisenhower present, where he signs a treaty for bases and technology exchanges. (Cooper also described the Grays, who work for the Large-nosed Grays, and a red-haired humanlike type, the "Orange.") By 1955 the aliens are cheating: mutilations for glandular secretions, enzymes, blood, and genetic experiments; unreported abductions; abductees not returned; and the discovery that the Large-nosed Grays created the human race and had long manipulated it "through religion, secret societies, witchcraft, satanism, magic, and the occult." MJ-12 continued diplomacy while developing Project Joshua (a low-frequency sonic cannon to destroy saucers) and Project Excalibur (a missile with a 1-megaton warhead able to penetrate 1,000 meters of hard-packed soil to destroy alien bases), with overtures to the Soviet Union. Peebles notes in an endnote that "Project Joshua" sounds like the weapon in the film *Earth vs. the Flying Saucers*, and that "Excalibur" was the code name for an X-ray laser undertaken as part of SDI.
- **Cooper's space program** — A 1957 study predicted Earth could not support human life by 2000 through overpopulation and pollution, producing Alternatives 1, 2, and 3, with the U.S./Soviet leadership choosing 2 and 3, and AIDS claimed as part of the population-reduction effort. Cooper said the Moon has seasonal plant growth, clouds, and an atmosphere dense enough for humans with only an oxygen tank; that a joint alien/U.S./Soviet Moon base already existed by the time Kennedy announced the landing goal; that a probe landed on Mars on May 22, 1962, confirming it could support life; and that whole cities existed on Mars by the late 1980s. The "official" space program was a cover story: Aurora went into orbit while alien-supplied UFOs carried humans to the Moon, Mars, Venus, and other planets.
- **Cooper's financing and assassinations** — MJ-12 cornered the illegal drug market, with "the ruling powers" approaching "an oil company president named George Bush"; "the CIA now controls all the worlds [sic] illegal drug markets." A second source was "a public charade of antagonism" between the U.S. and USSR, "when in fact we are the closest allies," with defense appropriations secretly diverted to MJ-12. MJ-12 ordered Kennedy's murder when he threatened to reveal the drug sales and the alien presence — Cooper claimed Kennedy's Secret Service driver turned around and shot him, and that all witnesses near enough to see were murdered within two years. MJ-12 ordered Nixon to resign over Watergate and, when he refused, launched a military coup to force him out; the Warren Commission, Rockefeller Commission, Church hearings, and Iran-Contra hearings were cover for the MJ-12 power structure.
- **Tracing Cooper's sources** — Cooper said he learned all this in the Navy during 1970–1973, but Peebles traces the elements to Bennewitz and Doty: the alien treaty, the secret bases, the Dulce firefight (which Cooper dated 1969, still with sixty-six casualties), and the Holloman landing. Moseley called the Lear/Lazar/Cooper stories "wild ravings"; CUFOS suggested they were a new kind of contactee story — claiming to have seen secret documents about aliens rather than to have met them. Cooper's inhabited Moon is "straight out of Adamski," and "Alternative 3" came from a BBC "April Fools" documentary that some UFOlogists took seriously.
- **Vallee's objections** — Jacques Vallee found it absurd that a civilization billions of years ahead of ours could not synthesize an enzyme, and noted Chicago slaughterhouses would gladly sell cattle organs. Lear's cattle-blood transfusion claim was ridiculous: a person would die within minutes of immunological shock. Told the Dulce base was "the size of Manhattan," Vallee asked, "Who takes out the garbage?" — Peebles adds, "(A better question would be 'Where's the Dirt?')" — since a base that size would produce waste, require water, and have massive heat output even underground. Vallee nonetheless called the stories "some of the best horror stories I had heard since my childhood days when my mother read me Grimm's Fairy-Tales."
- **The Ultra-Right reading** — Cooper's appeals to the "Constitution" and "true patriots"; MJ-12 secretly undermining America with drugs and gun control; martial law and suspension of the Constitution planned; a "secret alien army of implanted humans" (one in forty of the population) and "all dissidents" to be sent to one-mile-square concentration camps already built and rehearsed in 1984 under code name "REX-84." Peebles: "Just substitute 'Communist Conspiracy' for 'MJ-12' and one has the standard Ultra-Right claims of the 1950s." Cooper said the Council on Foreign Relations and Trilateral Commission "not only control but own this country" — major foundations, all major media and publishing, all major corporations, the upper echelons of government, and the largest banks — controlled in turn through the Bilderburgers, which control a network of banks and holding companies "with Jewish-sounding names." His drawings of the "Large-nosed Grays" were compared to racist stereotypes of Jews in Nazi posters, and his book *Behold a Pale Horse* includes the text of the *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*.
- **Cooper's 1991–1992 shift** — He dropped the UFO material, now claiming it was a hoax to create an "external threat" for setting up a World Dictatorship under the U.N.; within two years a "New World Order" would be established and U.S. sovereignty lost; the "saucer technology" was really based on captured Nazi saucers. Religious elements persisted: the Nordics compared to angels, the Grays to demons, and of the alien treaty — "After all, the Bible talks about a pact with the Devil in the last days, after Israel is reinstated. Leading to Armageddon."
- **The Ultra-Left reading** — CIA involvement in drug sales, rule by a small rich and powerful group, and the round-up of "Dissidents" all being Ultra-Left staples; the idea that modern weapons technology came from outside appealing to those who came of age in the sixties, with several UFOlogists supporting the alien myth having backgrounds as "Peace Activists"; and the right-wing-conspiracy reading of the Kennedy assassination, with Cooper's MJ-12 roster (Rockefeller, the CFR, the "Eastern Establishment") doubling as Ultra-Left bogeymen — "(Extremes do tend to meet.)"
- **The *UFO* magazine article** — Suggested links among the "October Surprise," the Iran-Contra arms deal, the BCCI bank scandal, the Lockerbie airliner bombing, Area 51, and the antigravity theories of T. Townsend Brown, NICAP's original founder, claiming a "war" between a CIA group called "Aquarius" (around a "power center" called MJ-12) and a Naval group, "COM-12," trying to preserve constitutional government. Peebles notes the source was in jail awaiting trial on drug charges.
- **The underground bases** — Beyond Dulce and Groom Lake, sites named include Albuquerque, Santa Fe, Taos, Sunspot, and Roswell, New Mexico; Colorado Springs; Catalina Island; Mount Shasta; the Nevada nuclear test site; and the Tejon Ranch area near Edwards AFB — described as underground cities with millions of aliens, implied to be a significant percentage of the official U.S. population. Peebles maps them onto the Labyrinth (prison of a monstrous human/"alien" hybrid) and Hell (misshapen beings with supernatural powers, willing human helpers, great rewards, and eternal damnation), and notes real counterparts — NORAD inside Cheyenne Mountain, presidential nuclear bunkers, underground nuclear testing in Nevada near Area 51 — plus the base named "Dreamland" and the science-fiction imagery of *Fantastic Voyage*, *Forbidden Planet*, *Wargames*, *The Andromeda Strain*, *Fail Safe*, and *The Time Tunnel*'s car diving into the ground where "the 'billion dollar time machine' is hidden."
- **"The Alien Myth 1987-1993"** — Peebles's closing summary of the myth's finished form: disk-shaped alien spaceships seen for thousands of years, with maneuverability and speed beyond earthly aircraft, leaving pad prints, causing blackouts, stopping cars, and crashing with debris and live and dead aliens; the "Grays," short, gray-skinned, large bald heads, thin elongated limbs, physically weak, sickly, genetically disordered; abducting humans to remove sperm and ova for alien/human hybrids, implanting noses to allow control, with millions of abductees whose memories are erased; mutilating cattle and humans for an enzyme needed as food; MJ-12's pact with the Grays supplying technology used to build the B-2, F-117, and Aurora in exchange for huge underground bases, mutes, and abductions, financed by CIA and George Bush drug sales; MJ-12, the Council on Foreign Relations, the Trilateral Commission, the Bilderburgers, and international bankers forming a "Secret Government" controlling every aspect of society, policy, and economics; murder used to remove obstacles, including President Kennedy and assassination witnesses; and a planned one-world dictatorship under a "New World Order," with abductees and others rounded up into concentration camps.
- **The MJ-12 name, from Peebles's endnote** — Peebles states the inspiration for "MJ-12" is unclear and lists possibilities: a classified Air Force publication whose title stands for "Meaconing, Intrusion, and Jamming Incidents" (meaconing meaning "Masking Beacon," the use of a powerful radio beacon to lead aircraft astray) [OCR: the scan prints the title as "MIJ? Quarterly"; the expansion Peebles gives yields the initials M-I-J-I]; "KH-11," a photo reconnaissance satellite camera; "MI 5" and "MI 6," which Peebles glosses as the British counterparts to the FBI and CIA; and the 5412 Committee.
- **The FBI question, from Peebles's endnote** — Klass's June 25, 1989, white paper notes the FBI questioned Moore on March 1, 1989, about the supposedly classified Project Aquarius Document; the FBI report stated "Moore declared that he had never had in his possession any classified documents from any government agency," although the document was stamped Secret, carried secret designations in the text, and was not to be downgraded until November 17, 2020. Peebles's conclusion: if Moore believed it real, he lied to the FBI; if not, "he was part of a hoax as early as 1981-1982." A related endnote records that *Just Cause* found Moore's decision to use the MJ-12 material in a novel suspicious — the real "Deep Throat" had provided solid information that could be checked and proven, whereas "[t]he novel smacked only of a money making deal to cash in on what was then a poorly-developed story" — and its reaction to the plan: "Do we have a situation where we have a fiction novel based on 'fact' which itself is based on fiction, which fiction would later be declared fact based on documents which seem to be based on fiction!!!"

## Thread
This is the chapter where Peebles's history turns into an argument about how myths metastasize. Having tracked the flying saucer myth's separate strands in earlier chapters — abductions, crashed saucers, and the mutes — he shows them fusing here into a single narrative with a documentary apparatus (MJ-12, Aquarius, the briefing papers) that gives it the surface texture of history, and then dismantles that apparatus piece by piece. Peebles's forgery analysis is the book's most sustained demonstration of its method: internal style, classification procedure, archival record, and physical evidence used to test documents rather than adjudicate belief. The chapter also supplies the mechanism by which the myth becomes unfalsifiable — the disinformation defense — and shows the political payload that arrives with it, ending with Cooper openly incorporating an anti-Semitic forgery. The concluding creed of the alien myth 1987–1993 sets up the next chapter, which asks what kind of times produce such myths.
